### Understanding 6061 Aluminum Types

6061 aluminum is one of the most widely used heat-treatable aluminum alloys because it offers a good balance of strength, corrosion resistance, weldability, and machinability. It is commonly used in construction, automotive parts, machinery, bicycle frames, and aerospace components.

The term **6061 aluminum** describes the alloy itself, while its **temper designation** describes how the material has been heat-treated or mechanically strengthened. Common types include **6061-O, 6061-T4, 6061-T6, and 6061-T651**.

- **6061-O:** Annealed and relatively soft. It is easy to form and bend but has lower strength.
    
- **6061-T4:** Solution heat-treated and naturally aged. It provides moderate strength with better formability than T6.
    
- **6061-T6:** Solution heat-treated and artificially aged. This is one of the most common forms because it offers high strength and good corrosion resistance.
    
- **6061-T651:** Similar in strength to T6 but stress-relieved by stretching. This helps reduce distortion during machining, making it popular for precision parts and plates.
    

In short, **6061-T6 is a strong, general-purpose choice**, while **6061-O and T4 are better when forming is important**. **T651 is particularly useful for large machined components where dimensional stability matters.**
