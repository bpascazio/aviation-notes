**2024 aluminum sheet** is one of the most famous and widely used high-strength structural metal alloys in the aerospace industry. Belonging to the 2xxx series, its primary alloying element is **copper**, which gives it exceptional strength-to-weight characteristics and remarkable fatigue resistance.

  

### Key Characteristics

- **High Strength:** 2024 offers high tensile and yield strength, making it ideal for structures that endure heavy loads and constant cyclic stress.
    
      
    
- **Fatigue Resistance:** Its standout engineering trait is its resilience to cyclic fatigue (the repeated flexing and stressing that aircraft parts experience during flight), which prevents cracks from rapidly propagating.
    
      
    
- **Common Tempers:** It is most commonly used in the **T3** temper (solution heat-treated, cold-worked, and naturally aged), which provides an optimal balance of high strength, damage tolerance, and formability.
    
      
    
- **The "Alclad" Version:** Because copper-alloyed aluminum is notoriously susceptible to corrosion, 2024 sheets are frequently supplied as **Alclad**. This means the high-strength core is metallurgically bonded to a thin layer of pure, corrosion-resistant aluminum on the outside surfaces.
    
      
    
- **Poor Weldability:** Unlike 6061 aluminum, 2024 is generally considered non-weldable (or very difficult to weld without cracking). Structures built with it are joined mechanically using rivets or specialized fasteners.
    
      
    

### Primary Uses

- **Aviation:** It has historically served as the benchmark material for **aircraft skins, fuselage panels, wing ribs, shear webs, and structural frames** across both commercial and general aviation.
    
      
    
- **High-Stress Transport:** It is also used in truck wheels, precision machinery parts, and gear where heavy-duty structural integrity is mandatory.
